A SAFER WAY TO CONNECT

Safety & screening

Practical steps for both sides. Verification and screening should always be transparent and respectful.

♢
IDENTITY, HANDLED WITH CARE

Identity verification isn’t set up.

This application does not collect ID or passport numbers, upload documents or verify users with government records. Profiles are not identity-verified.

A real service needs an authorised verification provider, informed consent, access controls, secure storage and a documented deletion process.

✓
A BETTER CONVERSATION

Ask job-related questions.

Discuss the duties, schedule, pay and relevant experience. Agree what references are appropriate before requesting contact details.

Never ask about someone's private life, medical history, religion or other protected personal information.

BEFORE WORK STARTS

Agree the important details together.

Confirm hours, duties, pay, breaks, transport expectations, household rules and how either person can raise a concern. Put the agreed details in writing.

KEEP YOUR ACCOUNT SAFE

Keep sensitive details private.

Do not share ID or passport numbers, passwords, banking information, home access codes or other private details through a household job post or message.

A LITTLE PREPARATION

Household-work essentials

PRACTICE, NOT A TEST

A few practical topics to discuss before accepting a household role. This guide is educational, not an employment screening decision.

  • Agree the work, schedule and pay before the first day.
  • Use cleaning products as directed; never mix products.
  • Agree an emergency-contact plan when caring for another person.
  • Ask for consent before sharing any personal information.
